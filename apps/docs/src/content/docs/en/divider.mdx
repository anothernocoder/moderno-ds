  intent: A rule that separates content.

  whenNotToUse:
    - case: A titled section of a page
      use: a heading, which carries the structure a rule only hints at
    - case: A border around one element
      use: that element's own border (Card, or the --border slot directly)
  gotchas:
    - "The label is the children/slot; with no children the divider is a bare rule."
    - "A bare divider is role=separator; a labelled one drops the role, because separator makes its children presentational and would hide the label."
    - "align only moves the label — it does nothing on a divider with no children."
    - "orientation=vertical takes its length from the row it sits in; give that row a height."
    - "A vertical divider with a label turns the caption sideways and widens the rule to fit it; the gap opens along the rule, above and below the caption."
  theming:
    - "The stroke is --border; the label is --muted-foreground. Never pass a colour."
    - "Spacing around the label comes from --spacing-2/--spacing-3; do not set margins inline."

Divider is CSS-only: there is no headless machine behind a rule. The two halves
of the line are the root's `::before`/`::after`, so a bare divider is one
continuous stroke and a labelled one splits around `[data-part="label"]` with no
extra markup. `data-orientation`/`data-align` from the recipe key every style in
`components.css`.
