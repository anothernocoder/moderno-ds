Field wires accessibility, not appearance: Ark's Field machine associates
`label[for]` with the control's `id` and mirrors `aria-invalid`/
`aria-describedby`/`aria-required` onto `data-invalid`/`data-disabled`/
`data-required` — those attributes, not CVA variants, are what
`components.css` keys the error/disabled look off.

The one thing you choose is `size`. It goes on `Field.Root`, and the single
`data-size` it emits sizes every part at once: the label, the control's height
and padding, and the helper/error text.

## Input and Textarea

Both controls are Ark parts of the same scope, so they share the border, radius,
focus ring and invalid treatment. They differ where the shape differs: an input
is one line with a fixed height, a textarea has a minimum height per size and
resizes vertically only, so it can never break out of the field's column.
