Card is the first compound primitive that is authored rather than re-exported:
there is no Ark machine for a surface — nothing to open, focus or wire — so
every part is ours. What makes it a Moderno primitive is the same contract
Button follows, widened across an anatomy: each part emits `data-scope="card"`
plus its own `data-part`, the root carries `cardRecipe`'s `data-variant` and
`data-size`, and `components.css` paints all of it from token slots.

## Anatomy

`root` is the surface; `header` holds `title` and `description`; `content` is
the body and the only part that grows, so a row of cards keeps its footers
aligned; `footer` is the actions row. Every part is optional and the order is
yours — whichever pair ends up adjacent shares one padding step instead of
stacking two.
