agent:
  intent: The body of a case study or customer story — a kicker, a serif heading, a lead, a list of project facts, titled sections of paragraphs with a pull-quote after the first one, and one call to action.
  whenToUse: "A page that tells how one project went: a portfolio case study, an agency's client story, a customer story on a product site."
  whenNotToUse:
    - case: A few paragraphs that explain something, with no project behind them
      use: Content — one reading column of sections and a quote, no facts
    - case: Many short quotes from different customers
      use: Testimonials — a grid of quote cards
    - case: The intro at the top of a personal portfolio
      use: PortfolioHeader — a photo, a name, a role and links
  gotchas:
    - "`facts` is an array of `{ id, label, value }`, shown as a description list: `label` is the `dt`, `value` the `dd`. Pass `[]` to hide the list."
    - "`sections` is an array of `{ id, heading, paragraphs }`. Each section renders an `h3` and one `p` per string in `paragraphs`."
    - "The pull-quote sits after the first section. `quote` is `{ text, author, role, initials, avatarUrl? }`; the Avatar shows `initials` until `avatarUrl` loads. Pass `null` to hide the quote."
    - '`kicker`, `lead` and `action` hide when you pass `""`.'
    - '`sections={[]}` shows "This case study has no story yet." in place of the story, and hides the quote and the action. The facts stay.'
    - "Presentational: `onAction` and `onRetry` report which button was pressed. Opening a page and retrying stay yours."
    - "`loading` keeps the kicker, heading and lead, and replaces the facts and the story with skeleton lines in one busy `status` region."
    - '`error` keeps the kicker, heading and lead, and puts an error Alert with your message and a "Try again" button where the facts and the story were. An empty string counts as no error.'
    - "The layout follows the block's own width, not the viewport: the lead steps up and the quote moves inward from `--container-sm`; the heading and the quote step up and the facts go to one row from `--container-md`; the facts move beside the story, with more room above and below, from `--container-lg`. The block is capped at `--container-lg`."
  theming:
    - "The block paints three rules of its own: the quote's left rule (--primary), the lines above and below the facts (--border) and the dashed empty box (--border). The speaker is Avatar's md size, the action is Button's outline variant, the error is Alert's error variant and the loading placeholders are Skeleton."
    - "The heading and the quote read --font-serif and --foreground; the facts' values, the section headings and the speaker's name read --foreground; the kicker, the lead, the facts' labels, each paragraph and the speaker's role read --muted-foreground."
    - "Type sizes come from the contract scale: text-heading-sm and text-heading for the heading, text-body-lg and text-heading-sm for the quote, text-body and text-body-lg for the prose, text-ui-sm and text-ui-md for the kicker, the facts and the speaker. The three steps are --container-sm, --container-md and --container-lg."

## API Reference

### Props

| Prop       | Type                    | Default                                     | Description                                                                          |
| ---------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `kicker`   | `string`                | `"Case study"`                              | A few words above the heading; `""` hides them.                                      |
| `heading`  | `string`                | `"How Northwind closes its books in a day"` | The case study's heading. Set in the serif face.                                     |
| `lead`     | `string`                | a sentence                                  | The summary under the heading; `""` hides it.                                        |
| `facts`    | `LongFormFact[]`        | four sample facts                           | The project at a glance: client, industry, team, timeline. `[]` hides the list.      |
| `sections` | `LongFormSection[]`     | three sample sections                       | The story, in order. `[]` shows the empty message.                                   |
| `quote`    | `LongFormQuote \| null` | a sample quote                              | The pull-quote after the first section; `null` hides it.                             |
| `action`   | `string`                | `"Read the next case study"`                | The outline button's label under the story; `""` hides it.                           |
| `error`    | `string`                | —                                           | Loading the case study failed; an error Alert with a retry replaces facts and story. |
| `loading`  | `boolean`               | `false`                                     | Skeleton lines in a busy region replace the facts and the story.                     |
| `disabled` | `boolean`               | `false`                                     | The content stays and every button is disabled.                                      |
| `onAction` | `() => void`            | —                                           | The button under the story was pressed. Svelte: `onaction`. Vue: `@action`.          |
| `onRetry`  | `() => void`            | —                                           | "Try again" after `error`. Svelte: `onretry`. Vue: `@retry`.                         |

### Fact

| Field   | Type     | Description                                    |
| ------- | -------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| `id`    | `string` | A stable key for the fact.                     |
| `label` | `string` | What the fact is, like `"Client"`. The `dt`.   |
| `value` | `string` | The fact itself, like `"Northwind"`. The `dd`. |

### Section

| Field        | Type       | Description                           |
| ------------ | ---------- | ------------------------------------- |
| `id`         | `string`   | A stable key for the section.         |
| `heading`    | `string`   | A few words. Rendered as an `h3`.     |
| `paragraphs` | `string[]` | The section's text, one `p` per item. |

### Quote

| Field       | Type     | Description                                      |
| ----------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| `text`      | `string` | The quoted words, in the serif face.             |
| `author`    | `string` | Who said it.                                     |
| `role`      | `string` | Their role and company, under the name.          |
| `initials`  | `string` | Shown in the Avatar until the photo loads.       |
| `avatarUrl` | `string` | Optional. A photo of the speaker for the Avatar. |

### States

| State    | Prop            | What renders                                                                 |
| -------- | --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Default  | —               | Kicker, heading, lead, the facts, every section, the pull-quote, the action. |
| Empty    | `sections={[]}` | Kicker, heading, lead, the facts and "This case study has no story yet."     |
| Loading  | `loading`       | Kicker, heading, lead and placeholders for the facts and two sections.       |
| Error    | `error`         | Kicker, heading, lead, then an error Alert with "Try again".                 |
| Disabled | `disabled`      | The same content with the action disabled.                                   |

### Sizing

| Container step           | What changes                                                            |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| below `@sm`              | The lead at body size; the facts in two columns.                        |
| `@sm` (`--container-sm`) | The lead steps up a size; the quote moves inward.                       |
| `@md` (`--container-md`) | The heading and the quote step up a size; the facts sit in one row.     |
| `@lg` (`--container-lg`) | The facts move beside the story; more room above and below the section. |

## Notes

- Three sections read best: the challenge, the approach, the outcome.
- Keep the facts short: one or two words each, four at most.
- Quote a person by name. A real name and role make the story believable.
